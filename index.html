🎮 Privacy Policy for Pocket Retro 90s Game

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 • Last Updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game application Pocket Retro 90s Game ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our"), available on the Google Play Store.

We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining user trust. This document outlines what data is processed when you enjoy our retro LCD handheld arcade game.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Pocket Retro 90s Game does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) directly from its users:

  • No account registration or login is required.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or mailing addresses.
  • We do not access your device's camera, microphone, contact list, gallery, or precise GPS location.

2. Local Storage and Game Progress

All your high scores, selected games, speed levels, sound settings, and game state are saved strictly locally on your device using standard Android private storage. This data never leaves your device and is permanently removed if you uninstall the app or clear app data in your phone's settings.

3. Device Permissions Used

Pocket Retro 90s Game declares and uses only the minimal required permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for delivering banner and interstitial ads through Google AdMob.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Enables checking connection status before requesting advertisements.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Triggers tactile haptic feedback when pressing control buttons and during in-game collisions.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

The app displays advertisements served by Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC.

Google AdMob may collect and process device telemetry and identifiers to deliver relevant ads, prevent click fraud, and evaluate ad performance. This data may include:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID)
  • IP address (used to infer broad geographic location such as city or region)
  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
  • Ad view and interaction statistics

You can learn more about how Google handles ad data or adjust your ad settings at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • You can manage or opt out of personalized ads directly on your Android device: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or delete advertising ID.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Pocket Retro 90s Game is an arcade gaming experience suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal data has been inadvertently collected, please reach out to us and we will promptly investigate and delete it.

6. Security

Your local gameplay preferences are stored in Android's isolated app sandbox. Any communication with third-party advertising networks utilizes standard secure encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy. Any changes will be updated on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
